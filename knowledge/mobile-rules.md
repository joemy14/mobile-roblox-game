# Mobile rules for kids games

Audience: kids 6 to 12 on phones and tablets, often using one hand.

- Buttons are big: at least 25% of screen width and 10% of screen height.
- Use at most 4 words per label. Use emoji or icons when possible.
- Main action buttons go at the bottom center, within thumb reach.
- Maximum 6 buttons on one screen.
- Every tap gives feedback: a small squash animation and a sound.
- Use bright colors with high contrast and a dark outline.
- No chat, no outside links, no countdown timers that push players to buy.
