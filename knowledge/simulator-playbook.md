# Simulator game playbook

Core loop: tap to collect coins, spend coins to upgrade, unlock a new area, repeat.

First 60 seconds:
- Show one big tap button right away.
- The first tap gives coins, with a sound and a number pop-up.
- The first upgrade costs about 20 coins, so it's affordable within 20 seconds.

Hooks that keep kids playing:
- Numbers that grow quickly.
- Pets or helpers that earn coins while the player is away.
- A daily reward with a streak bonus.

Progression: a new area every 5 minutes or so, each one visually different.
